# Asking a creator for clip permission

Copy, paste into their business email / DM, and save their yes (screenshot or email) somewhere you can find it.
When they agree, write a short note in the `permission` column of `creators/registry.csv`
(e.g. "email OK from <name> 2026-11-19"). The clipper starts using them on the next scan.

> Hi <name>, I run a GTA 6 clips page on TikTok and YouTube Shorts. I'd like to clip moments from your GTA 6 videos/streams,
> credit you in every caption with a link back to the original, and remove any clip on request.
> Is that okay? A reply of "yes" is all I need.

Tip: many creators list a business email in their channel's About tab.
